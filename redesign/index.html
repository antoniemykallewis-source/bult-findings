Ask BULT a question
What exactly is BULT?+

A once-daily dietary supplement system for men. One daily pouch contains five capsules and two softgels across CORE, VITALITY and OMEGA. It brings foundational nutrition, male vitality support and omega-3s into one routine.

How do I take it?+

Take the entire contents of one pouch daily with a meal and water. Follow the current label. If you have a medical condition, take medication or are unsure whether an ingredient is suitable, ask your healthcare professional before use.

What’s inside each pouch?+

Two CORE capsules, three VITALITY capsules and two OMEGA softgels. OMEGA contains fish from anchovy. and review the current product label for all ingredients and allergens.

Are all ingredient doses disclosed?+

Most ingredients have individual amounts listed. The prebiotic + probiotic blend is a combined 100 mg; individual blend amounts are not disclosed. We keep that distinction visible so you can make an informed choice.

Can I skip, pause or cancel?+

BULT’s current subscription offer allows you to skip, pause or cancel through your account. Your first order is $69, then $89 every 30 days until cancelled. Review the current subscription terms before purchasing.

What if it isn’t right for me?+

BULT advertises a 30-day first-order money-back guarantee. Its published refund policy has different conditions, so the final terms need to be reconciled before this concept goes live. Contact support@bult.com to confirm your eligibility. .
